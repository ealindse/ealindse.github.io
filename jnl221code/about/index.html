Elizabeth Lindsey is a current sophomore at Syracuse University. She is pursuing a dual major in Journalism on the Magainze, News, and Digital track and International Relations. She is from Greenville, South Carolina. She has an Associate in Arts from Greenville Technical College and graduated high school from the South Carolina Governor's School for the Arts and Humanities, where she studied creative writing.

She loves weird things, cappucinos, NYT mini games, and the beach. She has a poem published in Elan Magazine and is a current staff writer for the Daily Orange. She can't wait to read more, learn more, and write more as a future journalist and current citizen of the world. Her goals include being a yoga instructor, successfully cooking a meal that isn't burnt, and making work that promotes inclusivity, peace, and truth. Feel free to email her book recomendations or read her substack.
